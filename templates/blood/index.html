Who can donate?

Are you eligible to donate?

Most healthy adults can donate. Here are the basic requirements - the medical team confirms everything before each donation.

  • Age between 18 and 65 years
  • Weight of at least 45 kg
  • At least 90 days since your last donation
  • In good health, with no fever or active infection
